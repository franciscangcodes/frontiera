About Frontiera: Web Development & Designer in Metro Manila

My name is Francis, I am the owner and lead developer of Frontiera Digital Solutions. I started programming and designing web pages back in the old days of Friendster where you could make your own profile page standout by using custom codes. Ever since that, my love for programming and designing just grew fonder. I started making projects to automate the boring stuffs. My first ever real project was a payroll system I sneakily created using a company computer while working as a time keeper for a construction company. And now, I created Frontiera so that I could use those skills to help business owners have their own 24/7 store front by building websites that resonates their business.

Most of the business owners I have talked to expressed two things:

  • Having a website does not even cross their mind.
  • Websites are way expensive.

Frontiera’s goal is to bridge those gaps so businesses could thrive and compete in this modern digital era. We want business owners to see how having a website helps their brand get found, gain customers, and have a 24/7 storefront working for them while they sleep. All of this without breaking the bank and providing flexible payment options.

We are not some digital agency that doesn’t care about your business’ financial situation, we will make sure that you could establish your digital footprint without costing an arm and a leg.

What's with the name Frontiera?

Honestly? Frontiera wasn't the first name that came to my mind. I was really locked in on naming it Sunlounger Web Solutions.

It started one ordinary evening. While we were walking two of our dogs, and for once, we decided to take a different route than usual. Half-joking, I said, "A new frontier, then. Maybe we'll discover a new colony or finally meet a Borg." And there it was, the famous line: space, the final frontier. Our shared love for Star Trek is where all of this began.

But the name carries more than one story. As a kid, I spent countless hours in Ragnarok Online, where the legendary city of Prontera served as the game's central hub for trading, meeting, and adventuring. And then there's punterya, a Tagalog word meaning aim, goal, or target.

Put them together, and you get Frontiera: Where we aim to help MSMEs navigate the modern era and discover what's possible for their business.

To be visible, you must first deal with the invisible.

Creating driven-to-convert websites for businesses, big or small. 100% hand-coded, not vibe-coded.
